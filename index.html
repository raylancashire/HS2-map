Queen’s Park Affected Houses

HS2 bored tunnel reference through Queen’s Park, with properties loaded live from Google Sheets

Important information

This map shows homes where new cracks and/or subsidence have been reported as potentially associated with HS2 construction, alongside properties recorded as not affected.

Reports are based on recorded observations. Inclusion on the map does not establish that HS2 caused any reported cracking or subsidence. Distances to the tunnel alignments are estimates.
